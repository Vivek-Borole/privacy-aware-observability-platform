# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration space.
Participate in good faith, focus feedback on the work, respect differing
experience levels and backgrounds, and do not share another person’s private
information without permission.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private data or credentials, and
disruptive conduct that prevents others from participating.

Report concerns privately through the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove
content or restrict participation to protect contributors and users.
